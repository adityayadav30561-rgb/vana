How we think

Purpose behind every detail.

Vana is an independent studio for search, web and mobile. Our name is the Sanskrit word for forest — a reminder that lasting growth comes slowly, from deep roots, toward the light.

We are a small team of SEO specialists, designers and engineers who prefer depth to noise. We partner with founders and teams who care how things feel as much as how they perform, and we stay with them long after launch.

Our philosophy

  1. i.

    Clarity before craft

    We find the one true thing a brand should say, then design everything around it.

  2. ii.

    Nothing ornamental

    Every detail has to earn its place. Restraint is a design decision.

  3. iii.

    Built to grow

    Systems, not one-offs — work that stays alive as your company changes.

The journey

Four stages, walked together. Follow the feather.

  1. Discover

    We immerse ourselves in your world — interviews, audits, field visits — until we understand what makes you worth choosing.

    • SEO & site audit
    • Keyword research
    • Competitor landscape

  2. Define

    We distil what we've learned into a clear plan: the searches worth winning, the product worth building, and how it all fits together.

    • Search strategy
    • Site architecture
    • Product roadmap

  3. Create

    Websites, apps and content take shape together, crafted in close collaboration and tested with real people.

    • Websites & apps
    • Custom software
    • SEO content
